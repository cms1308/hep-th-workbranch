# Prose in papers by Jaewon Song

Analysis dated 2026-10-13. The most useful recurring feature in this sample is a guided argument: identify the task, perform a construction or calculation, show the result, and explain what it supports. First-person plural, concrete examples, and explicit qualifications make the reasoning easy to follow. These are observations about selected passages, not features proved unique to this author.

## Observations from the texts

**Introduce an object and give the reader an operation to perform.** The first solo paper moves from a stated sectional objective to a Lagrangian example, an index expression, and a transformation of that expression. “Let us consider” introduces the construction. In successive examples, the setup changes while the vocabulary and comparison remain stable. The reader follows the same argument without repeatedly learning a new description of the method. The paper also gives checks their own subsection. [1509.06730, §2.1](https://arxiv.org/html/1509.06730v3#S2.SS1), [§2.2.1](https://arxiv.org/html/1509.06730v3#S2.SS2.SSS1), [§4.2.1](https://arxiv.org/html/1509.06730v3#S4.SS2.SSS1).

**State the obstruction alongside the proposal.** The second solo paper's introduction develops the extra grading, then says, “This makes our prescription incomplete.” The limitation is specific: the general assignment needs information from four dimensions. The technical exposition proceeds through explicit states and counting rules; §3 explains the extra complication relative to §2. The discussion turns unresolved parts into concrete questions. This supports qualified, explanatory prose, with short transitions around dense mathematics rather than a fixed sentence-length target. [1612.08956, §1](https://arxiv.org/html/1612.08956v3#S1.p5.1), [§2](https://arxiv.org/html/1612.08956v3#S2), [§3](https://arxiv.org/html/1612.08956v3#S3), [§5](https://arxiv.org/html/1612.08956v3#S5).

**Narrate the logical calculation.** The Maruyoshi–Song letter constructs the theory in §II and walks through successive corrections in §III. “Let us redo” marks a necessary recalculation. The comparison of the resulting data leads to “Therefore we claim”. Thus a claim need not always be the paragraph's first sentence: its position follows the reasoning. The discussion names unresolved mechanisms and possible applications. [1606.05632, §II](https://arxiv.org/html/1606.05632v3#S2), [§III](https://arxiv.org/html/1606.05632v3#S3), [§V](https://arxiv.org/html/1606.05632v3#S5).

**Let the scale of the paper determine its exposition.** The landscape paper has a broad introduction, a quantitative abstract, a methods section, and detailed cases. In §4.5, “Let us look at the spectrum” opens a concrete inspection; a later paragraph explains a difficulty with the candidate interpretation. The final discussion returns to the symmetry-identification assumption. This coauthored example supports retaining useful motivation and explicit methodological limitations, not automatically making every section short. [2408.02953, §1](https://arxiv.org/html/2408.02953v2#S1), [§4.5](https://arxiv.org/html/2408.02953v2#S4.SS5), [§5, final paragraph](https://arxiv.org/html/2408.02953v2#S5.p9.1).

**Keep the common procedure visible across cases.** The recent TQFT paper distinguishes empirical evidence from justification and states the chamber restriction beside its proposed formula. Subsequent examples use “Following the same procedure” and then provide the new expression. Section 3 begins by identifying the task and the difficult step. These are continuities with the solo sample, although neither their authorship nor a chronological evolution of Song's style follows from this comparison. [2511.11186, §2.3](https://arxiv.org/html/2511.11186v2#S2.SS3.p4.4), [next example](https://arxiv.org/html/2511.11186v2#S2.SS3.SSS0.Px2.p1.1), [§3](https://arxiv.org/html/2511.11186v2#S3.p1.1).

## Applying the observations

The following are drafting recommendations inferred from the sample.

1. Give a section a concrete task before a long calculation. Connect the task to the paper's central claim.
2. Use first-person plural for choices and operations. Use a direct declarative sentence for a definition or established fact; every sentence need not begin with the author.
3. Introduce a displayed equation by its role. After it, explain the consequence or the subtlety needed for the next step.
4. Distinguish a definition, a calculation, evidence, and a conjecture through precise verbs. Keep the tested range, assumptions, and unresolved step explicit.
5. Explain a procedure in one representative case. In later cases, retain the terminology, refer back, and identify what changes.
6. Use short transitions to make dependencies clear. Preserve longer sentences when their conditions belong together.
7. End a discussion with identifiable open problems arising from the argument.

## Illustration and reusable instruction

The following is an original template, not a quotation or a scientific result. Replace every bracket with established project content; omit any sentence the evidence does not support.

> We consider [theory] with [specified deformation]. Using [method], we obtain [result]. For [tested cases], this agrees with [comparison] through [order]. The agreement supports [conjecture]. A derivation of [remaining step] is still needed.

Its order is illustrative, not mandatory. A paragraph devoted to a definition or a proof needs a different ending.

For a future drafting request:

> Use the observations in this document as a prose reference. Preserve the manuscript's established claims, notation, assumptions, citations, and verification ranges. Guide the reader through the argument with concrete operations and short transitions. State the purpose of each calculation and the consequence of each main result. Explain a representative case, then keep later cases concise and comparable. Distinguish evidence from proof and name relevant limitations. Follow PAPER-STYLE.md and the project's decisions wherever they impose a stricter requirement. Compose original sentences; do not copy source passages or insert stock expressions mechanically.
