---
name: proofread
description: Check a paper or selected sections for scientific consistency, references, notation, terminology and readability. Fix authorized editorial defects and report unsupported scientific claims; use revise for a specified change.
---

Read `harness/PAPER-REVIEW.md` for shared scope, authorization, validation and saving,
and `PAPER-STYLE.md` for prose. Review the requested sections, or the active manuscript
when the user asks for a full read-through. Review-only requests produce findings.

## Read against the evidence

Read the passages in scope and the relevant results, notes and data they rely on.
Check definitions and references outside the selected passage when it depends on them.
Use an existing section outline to orient a structural review when helpful; do not
create or complete a paragraph map as a prerequisite.

Focus on defects that affect the reader or the scientific claim:

- Claims, assumptions and quantifiers agree with the supporting record. A finite
  expansion, restricted parameter range or numerical check is not an all-orders proof.
- Equations, tables, captions and text agree; notation is defined and consistent.
- References point to the right material; renames have not left inconsistent terms.
- The argument has its necessary steps and avoids contradictory or redundant passages.
  Definitions, motivation and transitions may contribute without making new claims.
- Explanations are useful, prose is readable, and internal work logs do not substitute
  for scientific exposition. Apply punctuation preferences with judgment.

If the draft and evidence disagree, investigate and report the scientific discrepancy;
do not invent a result or weaken a needed claim simply to eliminate the finding.
Use cite-check for new, changed or questionable attributions, or a requested full audit.
An unchanged citation is not automatically a new source-reading task.

## Correct and report

Apply authorized editorial corrections and their actual dependencies. Update the section
outline if its argument changed. Mark citation claims needing source rechecking STALE,
preserving unaffected ledger evidence. Optional detailed tracking follows PAPER-REVIEW.
Compile after source changes, check affected references and layout, and checkpoint.
Report the scope reviewed, meaningful findings and fixes, and remaining uncertainties.
Record genuine new standing decisions, not one rule per corrected sentence.
